🎯 Smart Color Selection System

How It Works:

The game now intelligently selects shooting bubble colors based on:

  • Accessibility Analysis: Bubbles on the edges and bottom rows are prioritized
  • Cluster Detection: Colors that form larger groups get higher weight
  • Direct Line of Sight: Bubbles you can hit directly are prioritized
  • Difficulty Scaling: Helper probability decreases as you level up

Difficulty Phases:

Learning (L1-5): 70% helpful colors
Mastery (L6-15): 40% helpful colors
Excellence (L16+): 20% helpful colors

🎮 Testing Instructions:

  1. Press C to toggle the debug overlay (bottom-left)
  2. The overlay shows which colors are most accessible
  3. Watch how often you get helpful colors at Level 1
  4. Use colorStats() in console for detailed stats
  5. Progress to higher levels to see difficulty increase

Key Features:
• Early levels give you colors that match edge bubbles
• System adapts when you're in danger zone
• Mercy mode activates after multiple failures
• Statistics track helpful vs random selections
