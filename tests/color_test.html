Improvements in the New Palette:

  • Red vs Pink: Pure red (#FF0000) is clearly distinct from deep pink (#FF1493)
  • Blue variations: Sky blue (#0080FF) and dark turquoise (#00CED1) are easily distinguishable
  • Yellow/Orange: Gold (#FFD700) and dark orange (#FF8C00) have clear contrast
  • Green: Single bright green (#00FF00) instead of confusing green/lime variations
  • Purple: Dark violet (#9400D3) provides strong contrast against other colors
  • Overall: Each color has unique hue, saturation, and brightness values for maximum distinction
