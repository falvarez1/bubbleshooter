Key Features Implemented

Chain Lightning Mechanics:
  • Initial impact destroys hit bubble
  • 2-3 primary arcs to nearest neighbors
  • 1-2 secondary arcs from each primary target
  • Prevents hitting same bubble multiple times
  • Excludes power-up bubbles from chain targets
Visual Effects:
  • Dark stormy bubble with electric core
  • Crackling electric arcs around the bubble
  • Animated lightning paths between targets
  • Electric destruction effects
  • Screen shake and flash effects
Audio Integration:
  • Main lightning strike sound on activation
  • Electric arc sounds for each chain
  • Pitch variation for secondary arcs

Testing Instructions

  1. Open the main game at index.html
  2. Play until a Chain Lightning power-up appears (dark stormy bubble with electric arcs)
  3. Shoot the power-up at a cluster of bubbles
  4. Observe the chain lightning effect spreading from bubble to bubble
  5. Verify that:
    • Lightning chains to nearby bubbles
    • Visual effects show lightning arcs
    • Bubbles are destroyed with electric effects
    • No modal dialog appears
    • Gameplay continues smoothly
